        FIND YOUR WAY

        City map

        White dot: you. Cream dots: named stops. Dark shapes: obstacles. Choose a stop for a direction marker, then walk or drive yourself. This is a bearing, not a clear route or an automatic ride.

        WORLD

        Pick a place.

        Pins mark the only places built so far. Each has its own live crowd and shared goal; your points carry with you. Land outlines only, no borders drawn. Made with Natural Earth (public domain).

          PASSPORT

          Landmark stamps.

          Walk up to a landmark to stamp it, and look for the little traveler hiding nearby. Stamps and travelers both reset when you refresh or close the game.

            MINIGAMES

            Pick a game.

            Three little games for two. Play against someone else who is around, or the clearly-labeled computer.

            MINIGAME

            Ludo-style race.

            Two players, four pieces each. Roll a 6 to bring a piece out, race round the board and home with an exact roll. Land on another piece to send it back (not on starred squares). A 6, a capture or reaching home gives another roll. Winner earns points: 25 against a person, 10 against the computer. This is a simplified round-track version, not the standard cross board. Active games are saved on the server.

              MINIGAME

              Echo Dance.

              Watch the dance moves, then tap them back in the same order. Both dancers get the same sequence each round; it grows by one move every round. If one of you slips, the other wins. Just for fun - no points. Games are not saved; a restart ends them.

              All games · Ludo-style race · Freeze Beat

                MINIGAME

                Freeze Beat.

                Musical statues. Dance while the beat plays - the instant it stops, tap FREEZE. Fastest reaction takes the round; freeze too early and you lose it. First to 3 rounds wins. Your reaction is measured on your own device from the moment the stop sign appears, and the stop is always shown on screen (sound is optional). Close calls within 120ms tie. Timing can vary with your device and connection; no stakes. Just for fun, no points. Games are not saved.

                  VOICE (BETA)

                  City voice room.

                  Experimental city voice. Configured room limit: not checked. Full-room capacity and cross-network audio are still being tested. Your microphone stays off until you tap Join and allow it in your browser; you can also join listen-only. Voice goes directly between players' devices, so the other people in the room (and their browsers) can see your network IP address, which can reveal your rough location and internet provider. It is not recorded or stored by this site, and a Cloudflare STUN server is used to find a route (it sees your IP address too). Anyone in the room can hear you, so be kind: you can mute or report anyone. Muting stops sending your voice but keeps your microphone connected, so your browser may keep showing its mic-in-use light until you leave voice. Reports are saved for the site owner to review. Some networks block direct connections, so you may not be able to hear everyone.

                    HOW TO PLAY

                    Collect as you explore.

                    Flowers, mangoes, jackfruit, umbrellas. Walk over anything with a number on it and the points are yours.

                    • 🌼 Flower - 5 points
                    • 🥭 Mango - 10 points
                    • 🍈 Jackfruit - 15 points
                    • ☂️ Umbrella - 25 points

                    Move with WASD or the arrow keys (Shift to run), drag to look around. Points count for your place and, if you picked one, your district.
